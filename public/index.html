Take the headphones off. Keep the take.

Sing and play with your monitors up, the way it feels in the room. Headphoneless removes the speaker sound your mic picks up, so the recording is just you.

How it works

  1. Play your track through the speakers

    Set your monitors at a level you'd actually enjoy. Headphoneless listens to exactly what's going out to them.

  2. Record like you're on stage

    Stand where it sounds good, hear yourself in the room, and perform. Your mic picks up both you and the speakers, and that's fine.

  3. Get a clean take

    It works out how the speaker sound reached your mic and subtracts it, leaving your voice or instrument on its own, ready to mix.

Why lose the headphones

You sing in tune
Hearing your own voice in the air, not squashed into your ears, makes pitch easier to hold.
It feels like a performance
No cable, no clamp on your head, no sweat. Move the way you would in front of people.
No click leaking into quiet parts
Headphones bleed too, and that bleed is hard to remove. Speaker sound is something Headphoneless knows exactly, so it can come out.
Everyone hears the same mix
Track a full band or a choir in one room without a headphone amp for each person.
